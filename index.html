01 WHO WE ARE

FILIPINO ROOTS.
GALWAY HOME.
ONE COURT.

The Galway Fighting Maroons are a basketball club created by a Filipino and based in Galway, Ireland.

Inspired by Filipino roots and a shared love of basketball, the Maroons bring people together in Galway—on the court and beyond it.
